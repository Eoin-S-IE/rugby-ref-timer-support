# Rugby Ref Timer Support

Rugby Ref Timer is an Apple Watch app for rugby referees, designed to help track match time, playing time, time-off periods, halves, and yellow-card countdowns.

# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 7, 2026

Rugby Ref Timer does not collect, store, or share personal data.

## Data Collection

Rugby Ref Timer does not require an account and does not collect personal information.

The app does not collect names, email addresses, location data, health data, contacts, photos, payment information, or usage analytics.

## Data Storage

Rugby Ref Timer stores match timing information locally on your Apple Watch while the app is in use.

This information is not transmitted to the developer or any third party.

## Third-Party Services

Rugby Ref Timer does not use third-party analytics, advertising networks, tracking tools, or external data services.

## Children’s Privacy

Rugby Ref Timer does not knowingly collect data from children.
